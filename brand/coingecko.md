## Short description (EN)
GRANO is the utility token of a traceable coffee and cocoa ecosystem on Polygon. It is used to pay for on-chain origin passports that help importers comply with the EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR), to reserve the harvest of specific farm plots, and to buy coffee and cocoa products. Tokens spent in the ecosystem are burned, and the supply is fixed at 100 million.

## Long description (EN)
GRANO connects coffee and cocoa producers, importers and consumers through a verifiable record of origin, using a single token to pay for the network's services.

Utilities:
- Origin passport: brands importing coffee or cocoa into the EU pay in GRANO for a digital passport per lot (plot geolocation, harvest, weight, deforestation check), recorded on-chain and verifiable via a QR code. 50% of the GRANO paid is burned.
- Adopt a plot: consumers lock GRANO to reserve the harvest of a specific plot and receive its coffee or cocoa at home. The producer is paid in advance.
- Shop: coffee, cocoa and chocolate with a discount when paying in GRANO; redeemed tokens are burned.
- Phase 2: micro-lot auctions for holders and community voting on an impact fund for cooperatives.

Token facts: fixed supply of 100,000,000 GRANO, no mint function, no admin keys on the token contract, burnable. Allocations are held in public time-locked vesting vaults (team: 12-month cliff + 36-month linear). All contracts are verified on Polygonscan.

GRANO does not grant rights to profits, dividends or buybacks and does not represent any quantity of coffee or cocoa.
